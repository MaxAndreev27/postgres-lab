# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting Issues

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository security policy. All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
